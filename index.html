About

I am an Embedded Systems Engineer and Electronics Developer with over six years of professional experience in firmware, hardware integration, and product development. My work spans embedded firmware, USB and BLE communication, industrial interfaces, real-time systems, power electronics, and Embedded Linux platforms.

I have developed solutions based on STM32, ESP32, Silicon Labs, Nordic, and other microcontroller platforms, working across the complete product lifecycle—from requirements analysis and architecture design to firmware implementation, hardware bring-up, testing, and deployment. My experience includes communication protocols such as USB, BLE, Modbus, RS-485, I²C, SPI, UART, and Ethernet, along with sensor integration, audio systems, imaging devices, and industrial automation applications.

Alongside commercial development, I hold an M.Tech. in Power System Engineering, with research focused on grid-connected power converters and control systems. This combination of embedded systems expertise and power electronics research enables me to work effectively at the intersection of hardware, firmware, control systems, and power electronics.

As an independent consultant and freelancer, I collaborate with startups, research groups, and companies to transform concepts into reliable engineering solutions, delivering practical designs that are maintainable, scalable, and production-ready.
