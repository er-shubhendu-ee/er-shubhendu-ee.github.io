Overview

BLE Streaming Platform

Development of a custom Bluetooth Low Energy platform for continuous data streaming between embedded devices and desktop/mobile applications. The platform was designed to support reliable transfer of sensor data, audio streams, image data, device telemetry, and control commands while operating within BLE throughput and power-consumption constraints.

The work included firmware development, GATT architecture design, communication protocol implementation, desktop application development, testing, debugging, and performance optimization.

Responsibilities

Key Contributions

  • Custom BLE service and characteristic design.
  • Firmware development on Silicon Labs BLE platforms.
  • Reliable notification-based streaming implementation.
  • Desktop application development using Python.
  • Audio acquisition, compression, and transfer.
  • Image capture and transfer over BLE.
  • Sensor and IMU data streaming.
  • Connection management and state-machine implementation.
  • Debugging using protocol analyzers and application logs.
  • Performance tuning for throughput, latency, and power consumption.

Architecture

System Design

The system consisted of a BLE-enabled embedded device communicating with desktop and mobile applications through custom GATT services. Data was organized into dedicated channels for control, telemetry, audio, image, battery monitoring, and sensor streaming.

The firmware implemented packet sequencing, buffering, transfer control, and event-driven processing to maintain reliable communication under varying radio conditions.

Technical Challenges

Engineering Focus Areas

  • Maintaining reliable high-rate notifications.
  • Managing BLE MTU limitations.
  • Fragmentation and reassembly of large data packets.
  • Audio streaming under limited bandwidth conditions.
  • Image transfer reliability and packet recovery.
  • Connection stability during extended operation.
  • Cross-platform interoperability testing.
  • Power optimization during streaming operations.

Outcome

Results

The platform successfully enabled continuous streaming of telemetry, sensor, audio, and image data between embedded devices and host applications. The resulting architecture provided a reusable foundation for future BLE-enabled products requiring reliable wireless data transfer and remote device management.
